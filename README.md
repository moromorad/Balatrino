# Balatrino 

## A unique card game

The aim of this project is to recreate the game "Balatro" by the developer LocalThunk **with some twists of my own.** It is a poker-themed roguelike deck-building game.
The game is played by playing cards that give you a score to defeat "Blinds" (a certain score that has to be achieved). You can get higher scores by playing certain poker hands, such as Pair, Two-Pair, Full House and Royal Flush.

You can also increase your score by having "Joker" cards which ewach have special abiliteis to enhance your score.
Every round, the player has a limited amount of hands that can be played, with each hand having a maximum amount of card that can be played.
The game ends if the player cannot reach the total score of the blind after playing all their hands.
Every third blind is a **Boss Blind** which is a blind that has a special perk such as "All diamond cards are debuffed" or "must play 5 cards per hand".
After every round, depending on the players performance (e.g. number of hands remaining), they recieve cash, which can be used in the shop, which can be accessed at the end of every round.

In the shop you can buy:
- Jokers
- Tarot cards (which offer unique perks)
- Planet cards (which can increase the score attained by playing a certain hand)
- other perks e.g. increase hand size

The aim of the game is to defeat as many Blinds as possible. 

The actual game is really fun and I felt like it would be a nice and interesting challenge to try and recreate it in my project.
